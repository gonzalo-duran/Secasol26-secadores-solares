# Glossary

Terms and symbols used in the interface and in this manual, in alphabetical order.
Units are the internal ones (SI) unless indicated.

## Terms {#terminos}

**Absorber** — the plate that captures the radiation in the collector and transfers
it to the air. Its absorptance $\alpha$ defines how much radiation it converts into
heat.

**Albedo ($\rho_g$)** — fraction of the radiation the ground reflects [0–1]. It
enters the reflected component of the [POA](?manual=radiacion#transposicion).

**Batch** — a drying batch: loaded, dried completely, unloaded.

**COP of drying** — evaporation energy obtained per unit of energy invested (useful
solar + auxiliary + motor).

**Cover** — the glazing of the collector or of the translucent faces of the chamber
(polycarbonate, glass, film). It transmits radiation ($\tau$) and curbs the
convective losses.

**CSTR** — *continuous stirred-tank reactor*: a perfect-mixing model used for the
chamber air (no internal gradients).

**DHI / DNI / GHI** — the three radiation components reported by the databases:
diffuse horizontal, direct normal and global horizontal. See
[Solar radiation](?manual=radiacion#componentes).

**Dry basis / wet basis** — two ways of expressing the product moisture: per kg of
dry matter ($X$) or per total kg ($w$). Secasol uses dry basis. See
[Drying physics](?manual=secado#base-seca).

**GAB** — a sorption isotherm model (Guggenheim–Anderson–de Boer) with parameters
$X_m, C, K$ per product. See [Drying physics](?manual=secado#isotermas).

**Main duct** — the manifold that collects the air from the array branches in large
scale. See [Technical · Industrial](?manual=tec-industrial#cano-maestro).

**Monthly typical day** — a 24 h day built by averaging hour by hour all the days
of a month. Basis of the Annual analysis mode.

**Picard loop** — a fixed-point iteration that closes the coupling between the
chimney temperature, the natural flow and the collector outlet. See
[Technical · Motor and chimney](?manual=tec-chimenea#picard).

**POA** — *plane of array*: irradiance on the tilted and oriented plane of the
collector, obtained by transposition from GHI/DNI/DHI. It is the radiation all the
collector calculations use.

**Psychrometry** — the thermodynamics of moist air. See
[Psychrometry](?manual=psicrometria).

**Semi-continuous** — a large-scale subtype: a counter-current tunnel where the
product advances through zones while the air circulates in the opposite direction.

**Set-point** — the target temperature of the auxiliary; the control delivers only
the power needed to reach it.

**Solar fraction** — the part of the total heat provided by the sun:
$E_{solar}/(E_{solar}+E_{auxiliar})$.

**Sorption isotherm** — the equilibrium relationship $X \leftrightarrow a_w$ at a
given temperature.

**Thermal draft** — the depression generated by a column of hot air (less dense)
that moves the air in natural convection.

**Warm-start** — starting a solver from the solution of the previous point (it
speeds and robustifies the node-by-node convergence of the collector).

**Water activity ($a_w$)** — the equilibrium relative humidity of the air in
contact with the product [0–1]. It measures how "available" the product's water is.
See [Drying physics](?manual=secado#actividad).

**Window (production)** — a real span [start, end] over which the multi-day
analysis is run with hourly climate.

## Frequent symbols {#simbolos}

| Symbol | Quantity | SI unit |
|---|---|---|
| $\beta$ | collector tilt | ° |
| $\gamma$ | azimuth (Duffie convention) | ° |
| $\theta_z$, $\theta_i$ | solar zenith / incidence angle | ° |
| $I_g$, $I_{POA}$ | global / collector-plane irradiance | W/m² |
| $\tau$, $\alpha$, $\varepsilon$ | transmittance, absorptance, emissivity | – |
| $T_f$, $T_{abs}$, $T_{prod}$, $T_{flujo}$ | temperatures (fluid, absorber, product, chamber air) | K |
| $\dot m$, $\dot m_{as}$ | mass flow (air, dry air) | kg/s |
| $\dot m_w$ | evaporation rate | kg/s |
| $\omega$ | absolute air humidity | kg water/kg dry air |
| $X$ | product moisture (dry basis) | kg water/kg dry |
| $a_w$ | water activity | – |
| $h_{conv}$, $h_w$, $h_{int}$ | convection coef. (product, wind, channel) | W/(m²·K) |
| $K_m$ | mass-transfer coef. | kg/(m²·s) |
| $U$ | global conductance | W/(m²·K) |
| $\Delta P$ | pressure drop | Pa |
| $Re$, $Nu$, $Pr$, $Ra$, $Le$, $Sh$ | dimensionless numbers (Reynolds, Nusselt, Prandtl, Rayleigh, Lewis, Sherwood) | – |
| $D_h$ | hydraulic diameter | m |
| $L_v$, $h_v$ | latent heat / vapor enthalpy | J/kg |
| $c_p$, $c_{p,h}$ | specific heat (air, moist air) | J/(kg·K) |
| $\rho$, $\mu$, $k$ | density, viscosity, conductivity | SI |
| $\varepsilon$ (bed) | porosity | – |
| $d_p$, $\Phi_s$ | particle diameter, sphericity | m, – |
